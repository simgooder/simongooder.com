The problem

Students are outsourcing their thinking.

When AI hands over the answer, students skip the part where learning happens.

Learning

Copy-and-paste answers mean the work gets finished and the thinking doesn’t happen.

Privacy

Student data often goes to AI companies the school has no relationship with.

Safety

When a student tells a chatbot they’re struggling, most tools have no plan.

Tutor mode

A tutor, not an answer machine.

  1. 1

    Meets you where you are.

    It asks what you know, gives a hint, or shows a similar example before it explains.

  2. 2

    Answers from approved sources.

    Every answer points back to the content your school has approved.

  3. 3

    Checks it stuck.

    After an answer, a quick task: recall it, explain it back, or try a new case.

Learning modes

The same lesson, six ways.

Quiz, diagram, flashcards, slideshow, article, or guide. Everyone gets the same approved content in the form that works for them. Student's choice.

Quiz

Students make their own quizzes from approved content. Answers explain why.

Diagram

Labelled diagrams and cycles built from approved sources, with a text description for screen readers.

Flashcards

Study cards and guides from approved content, ready for spaced practice.

Slideshow

Simple slide briefings a teacher can project and rewrite into their own template.

Article

A cited overview assembled from approved sources.

Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough, chunked, with optional narration.

Modes are in development and will be tested with pilot schools.

Trust

Built for safety and trust

Approved content only

Answers draw on licence-cleared material, with sources shown.

No third-party AI

Student questions never go to outside AI services.

Hosted in your region

Your data stays in Canada or the US, under a data-processing agreement.

Alerts your school

A student who needs help is connected to support and the school is notified.
