A safer way to bring AI into your schools.

Start small, learn with your teachers, and scale on your terms.

How adoption works

Three steps, no dates.

  1. 1

    Pilot

    A small group of teachers and students use a private, hosted instance in your region.

  2. 2

    Review

    You and your team look at what worked, using success measures you chose.

  3. 3

    Scale

    Expand to more classes and schools when you’re ready.

Roles and controls

You stay in control.

  • Roles for students, teachers, school admins, and IT
  • Aggregate reports and flagged messages; a student’s full conversation can be opened only for an investigation, with a stated reason
  • An audit trail of every staff access to a student record, searchable by your district
  • A designated safety contact receives alerts
  • You set retention and which content is on
Fits what you already use

Works with your systems.

Single sign-on through your school accounts. Roster import by file or student information system.

Integrations are planned and confirmed with each pilot school.

Hosting

In your region, or on-site.

Hosted in your region by default. Boards that need data on-site can ask about on-premises options.
